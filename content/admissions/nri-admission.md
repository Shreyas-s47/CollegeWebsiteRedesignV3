### NRI Admission Process For International / NRI Students

All the SEA Group of Institutions are located in a congenial atmosphere of Vast Gardens, Nursery, and open spaces. The Group offers a variety of Courses, providing for Multi-disciplinary opportunities for Learning. The colleges provide a safe and secure environment for all the students and faculty. International/NRI students wishing to seek admission at SEA Group of Institutions through the Management Admission Process are requested to contact the Admission Cell at the college campus directly or through Filling the Admission Form available on the website. The Admission office will take up further processing.

The selection process will be purely based on the academic merit and aptitude of the student. The students are not required to appear for any of the tests. The college will conduct an interview and assessment process prior to granting admission.

Enquiries Regarding Admission may be addressed via email/ phone
seaeduinfo@seaedu.ac.in +91 7353945999 / 080-29730681

#### Engineering

The following Academic eligibility criteria are required to be followed irrespective category of seats(Govt.and Management quota seats)

##### Eligibility for Admission to B.E.

Pass in PUC or 10 +2 equivalent examination with a minimum of 45% (40% for SC /ST and OBC groups) marks aggregate in Physics and Mathematics and any one of the following subjects-Biology / Chemistry / Computer Sciences / Electronics.

##### Scholarships

The Trust plans to institute/offer many scholarships under merit and merit-cum-means categories by the creation of a fund. It will also correspond with various agencies listed below for extending their schemes to the college for the benefit of the students. GOI, National Scholarship, Jindal, Community based Endowment Scholarships, Free ship, SC & ST Scholarship from Social Welfare Board, Alumni Scholarship/awards (after the emergence of the first batch).
