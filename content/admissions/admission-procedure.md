#### SEA College Of Engineering and Technology

All candidates must meet the program specific eligibility criteria. (Eligibility criteria for each program is on the admissions page of the specific program) Candidates meeting the eligibility criteria should follow the below-mentioned steps:

**Step 1**

Begin your journey by completing our easy online/offline application form. Provide us with your personal details, academic background, and any relevant documents required for admission.

**Step 2**

Once we receive your application, our admissions team will schedule an interview to get to know you better. This interview allows us to understand your interests, aspirations, and suitability.

**Step 3**

After a successful interview, submit any additional requested documents requested. These documents may include academic transcripts, letters of recommendation, or proof of identity.

**Step 4**

Finally, upon review of your application and supporting documents, our admissions committee will make a decision regarding your admission. If accepted, you will receive a formal confirmation
