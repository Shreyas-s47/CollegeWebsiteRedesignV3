## Admission

SEA GROUP as an institution believes in moving along with the time and in most cases are the torchbearers when it comes to adopting new techniques in education. Same goes in the case of even the admission procedures too at Krupanidhi. Students are often confused about which stream to choose and parents are worried about job opportunities and placement percentage of the college. When you try Krupanidhi you can be at peace as we can help you with both. Both parents and students can go through our site to check out the syllabus and placement details for all the courses that we offer and choose accordingly. In case you still need help, you can approach us directly with your doubts and we would guide you along.

For people who are comfortable with online transactions, you can check out how to apply online, after paying a nominal amount for the application fees. In our Admission Procedure, we would take you through a step by step process of how to go about it. You can choose to approach us at our regional centres to buy the application form and also read up on the course details.

We have a transparent admission process, if all the documents and mark sheets add up to our expectations, and the student has been chosen for the course they had applied for, then we would intimate you at the earliest through email or telephone.

Once you receive news about your admission, you will have to pay the course fee before the due date, failing which your admission would be considered void.

We at SEA College strongly believe in fair treatment of everyone who has applied to join our courses. Here is wishing you good luck and hoping you would soon be a part of the SOUTH EAST ASIAN family.
