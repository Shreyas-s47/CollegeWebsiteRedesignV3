#### Introduction

Nursing is a career filled with endless personal and professional rewards. If you choose nursing, you are choosing to spend your life helping other.s using skills that blend scientific knowledge with compassion and caring there are very few profession s that offer such a rewarding combination of high tech and high touch. bearing occurs i variety of settings and through a variety of delivery methods.

#### Course Plan

**Diploma in General Nursing & Midwifery (GNM) ( 3 years )

###### About Course

The SEA School of Nursing is affiliated to Karnataka State Diploma in Nursing Examination Board, Bangalore ( K.S.D.N.E.B), KNC & INC. GNM is a 3.6 years graduate study programme. The diploma programme is designed to provide students with the basic knowledge, skills and attitudes necessary to enter the health care systems as professional nurse. Students develop skills and judgement in assessing, planning, implementing and evaluating nursing care for all age groups. Particular purpose placed on the ability to deliver quality care in therapeutic manner.

###### Objectives

- Apply knowledge and skill necessary for assessing nursing needs of the individuals throughout the life cycle, families and communities.

- Apply relevant knowledge from biological and behavioural sciences in planning and carrying out safe nursing care activities.

- Cooperation with members of the health team in the hospital or in the community in prevention of diseases, promotions restoration of health & rehabilitation.

- Biological Science

- Behavioural Science

- Fundamentals of Nursing

- Community Health Nursing

- Medical Surgical Nursing I & II

- Mental Health Nursing

- Computer Education

- Midwifery and Gynaecology

B.Sc Nusring ( 4 years )

###### About Course

This is a 4years graduate study program which is affiliated to TGUHSm KNC & INC which has major emphasis on basic human sciences path-physiology changes scope of disease, treatment and research to develop a student as a professional nurse, educator, tressarder and nurse practitioner.

Scope of nursing practice encompasses provision of promotive preventive curative and rehabilitations aspects of care to people across their life span in wide variety of health care settings. Practice of nursing is based upon application of basic concepts and principles derived form the physical, biological and behavioural sciences, medicine and nursing.

Nursing is based on values of caring, and aims to help individuals to attain independence in self care, it necessitates development of compassion and understanding of human behaviour among its practitioners to provide care with respect and dignity and protect the rights of individuals and groups.

Under graduate nursing education program prepares its graduates to become exemplary citizen by adhering to code of ethics and professional conduct at all times in fulfilling personal, social and personal, social and professional obligations so as to respond to national aspirations.

M.Sc Nusring ( 2 years )

###### About Course

The programme prepares nurses for leadership position in nursing and health fields who can functions as nurse specialists, consultants, educators, administrators and researchers in a wide variety of professional settings in meeting the national priorities and the changing needs of the society. This programme provides the basis for the post mistral programme in nursing. further the programme encourages accountability and commitment fo life long learning which fosters improvement of quality care.

###### Objectives

On completion of the two years M.Sc Nursing programme, the graduate will be able to:

- Utilize/apply the concepts, theories and principles of nursing science

- Demonstrate advance competence in practice of nursing

- Demonstrate leadership qualities and function effectively as nurse educator and manager.

- Demonstrate skill in conducting nursing research, interpreting and utilising the findings from health related research

- Demonstrate the ability to plan and effect change in nursing practice and in the health care delivery system

- Demonstrate interest in continued learning for personal and professional advancement.

###### A Brief course Overview

Post Basic Nusring ( 2 years )

###### About Course

Post Basic B.Sc degree in nursing is a broad based education aimed to build upon the skills and competencies acquired at the diploma in nursing level. make independent decisions in nursing situations, protect the rights of and facilitate individuals and groups in pursuit of health, function in the hospital, community nursing serviced, and conduct research studies in the areas of nursing practice, they are also expected to assume the role of teacher, supervisor, and manager in clinical/public health settings.

The programme is intended to enable the graduates assume responsibility as professional competent nurse and midwife at basic level in providing promotive, preventive, curative and rehabilitate services. the graduates made independent decisions in nursing situations protect the rights of and facilitate individuals and groups in pursuit of health, function in the hospital community nursing services, and conduct research studies in the practice.
