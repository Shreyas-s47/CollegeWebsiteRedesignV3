## SEA Composite Pre-University College

( Accredited at “A” Grade by Karnataka PU Board )

### Course Details

- Duration 2 Years Full

- Campus KR Puram Bangalore

- Language English

- No. of Intake 100

- Website [www.seapu.edu.in](https://seapu.edu.in/)

- Contact +91 7338338707

- Prospectus Download

Recognised by Karnataka State PU Board, the SEA PUC Conducts the 2 years pre university course, for both boys and girls. The composite college conducts courses in Science, Commerce and Arts, Prescribed by the Karnataka State Pre-University Board. The college places emphasis on Interactive learning by nurturing excellence, has been able to successfully groom thousands go young brains into becoming the intellects of young India. The students passing out of this college have soon realised that the tenure had been fruitful and rewarding professionally and personally.

Education at SEA is not just a matter of learning by rote, but quite the opposite. In fact, the band of faculty and highly qualified professionals introduce the students to the threshold of their minds . They act as motivators and facilitators focusing on interactive approach to assure that education is appealing attractive and alive.
