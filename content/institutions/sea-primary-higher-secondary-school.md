SEA Primary and Higher Secondary School was established in the year 2001 by the Founder chairman Shri A Krishnappaji with the the great vision and mission of creating world class citizens by giving the best education at the nominal fee so that people of all economic levels can receive excellent education.

The name SEA defines

**S-** Super Sports**
E-** Excellent Education**
A-** Amazing Activities

Accordingly SEA Primary and Higher Secondary School which follows Karnataka state government syllabus provides quality education to the students .We have well qualified ,dedicated and trained teaching faculty who give individual care and motherly affection to the students. Every year we get the best result in SSLC board examination with top marks .The results in 2019 -20 was amazing with Prajwal M securing 620/625 and brought pride to the institution.

Our students who got qualified from us are well established and leading a successful life all over the world.SEA Primary and Higher Secondary School always aspire for the welfare of the students .We wish all our students very happy and successful future and welcome kids to join us to build the future
