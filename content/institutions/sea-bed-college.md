#### Eligibility

A citizen of India who has passed B.A. / B.Sc. Degree examination of Bangalore University or any University in India or aboard recognized as equivalent through 10+2+3 pattern of education obtaining 50% of marks in aggregate of all the subjects.

#### Duration

B. Ed Degree shall extend over a period of one academic year.
