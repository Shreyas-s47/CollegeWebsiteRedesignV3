S.E.A group of Institution feels proud and happy that they started ICSE syllabus in the year 2002 under the support and guidance of Late Shri. Krishnappa.

We started with the Pre-primary session in the year 2002 and gradually, as years passed by, classes increased till X std. The first batch of the students successfully completed X board exams in the year 2009. We get 100% results every year.

The barren land which was like a jungle changed its form to the present institution with spacious well-ventilated class rooms and has all the modern facilities. The entire campus is like a canopy. It has a vast playground where students are being trained in various sports activities.

The management only appoints trained Montessori teachers for Pre-school, trained graduate teachers for Primary and Middle school and trained Post Graduate teachers for High school.

The teacher-student ratio is 1:25 to ensure personal attention among the students. The timetable is framed in such a way that 2-3 periods are allotted for Extra Curricular Activities to train students in various activities.

There are enough number of buses with drivers who are very caring and responsible. Each bus has one Group-D lady who is responsible for the security of the students.
