Our campus features cutting-edge facilities and a sophisticated infrastructure. In terms of infrastructure, equipment, and modern amenities, the campus meets all of the minimum requirements specified by the governing institutions. The campus is intended to provide a desirable standard atmosphere for learning that is free of pollutants while remaining easily accessible.The SEA campus is filled with rich green environment, properly built internal roadways, and street lighting. Beautifully planted gardens on campus let students refresh their minds and bodies after courses.

The class rooms are well-designed and well-ventilated. Students and faculty can use well-equipped computer laboratories. Training sessions for both hardware and software are held on a regular basis. All labs are network-enabled and connected to the internet via a centralized server room.

The central library is well-stocked with books, magazines, journals, and newspapers. Students and faculty have their own reading rooms. There is also a computer zone, a microfilming space, a documentation center, and an audio visual room.

There are distinct accommodations for boys and girls on campus. A campus guest house accommodates visiting faculty and guests. For major events, an isolated auditorium is being erected. A gym, football and cricket fields, basketball, volleyball, and badminton courts are all designed to help our pupils burn off surplus energy.
