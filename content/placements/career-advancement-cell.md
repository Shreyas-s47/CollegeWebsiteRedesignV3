Students can apply either through an online application form or by sending the hard copy of the application form which will be scrutinized by the admission team. Then the applied candidate should write an essay stating the purpose in Statement of Purpose (SOP) interweaving the profile with future aspirations and goals.

Applicants will take interviews through skype or through GDPI at the campus, at their convenience. The esteemed panelists decide the selection based on the interview performance. The panel’s decision is final. If selected, the applicant will be asked to take a Zoom Info-session to have in-depth information about the college. The selected candidates will receive selection mail for registration which is the first step of the admission process.
